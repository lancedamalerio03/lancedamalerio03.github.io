Project Overview

Comprehensive educational analytics automation platform designed to process learner feedback from corporate training bootcamps and automatically generate professional Google Slides reports with AI-powered insights, enabling scalable analysis for any current or future educational program at Eskwelabs.

🎯 Project Purpose & Context

Developed during my Education Innovation Fellowship at Eskwelabs to revolutionize the manual reporting process that previously required days of work. The platform was tested and validated using data from previous learning sprints, proving its effectiveness in processing diverse educational feedback structures.

This scalable solution empowers Eskwelabs to automatically analyze feedback from any learning sprint format - past, present, or future - delivering consistent, professional reports that enhance stakeholder communication and enable data-driven educational improvements.

Built as a comprehensive Streamlit-based platform integrating advanced data processing pipelines with NLP analysis, automated visualization generation, and seamless Google Workspace integration. The system processes multiple data formats and survey structures while maintaining analytical rigor and professional presentation standards.

The platform delivers publication-ready reports in under 5 minutes, representing a 90%+ reduction in manual effort while enabling consistent analytical frameworks across diverse training programs and stakeholder requirements.
