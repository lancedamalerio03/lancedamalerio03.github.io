Project Overview

Commissioned project for Keepital's Industrial B2B Digital Marketplace - A comprehensive Streamlit-based application that combines traditional OCR technology with modern AI parsing to automatically extract and structure contact information from business card images, featuring specialized optimization for Philippine formats and seamless Google Sheets integration.

🏢 Client Project for Keepital

This project was commissioned by Keepital, Asia's leading Industrial B2B Digital Marketplace that bridges the gap between suppliers and buyers. The platform needed an automated business card digitization tool specifically optimized for the Philippine market to streamline contact management operations.

The solution eliminates manual data entry bottlenecks when onboarding suppliers and connecting with potential business partners, providing accurate contact extraction tailored to local business card formats and conventions.

This production-ready Streamlit application features a comprehensive multi-stage OCR pipeline: image preprocessing → EasyOCR extraction → GPT parsing → structured JSON output. The tool processes multiple business card images simultaneously, validates and structures the extracted data, and provides multiple export options including direct Google Sheets integration and CSV downloads.

Built with enterprise-grade requirements in mind, the application includes advanced features like configurable AI model selection (GPT-4o-mini, GPT-3.5-turbo, GPT-4, GPT-4o), real-time processing progress tracking, data preview with validation, and secure API key management. The system is specifically optimized for Philippine business card formats, including local phone number patterns, address structures, and naming conventions.
