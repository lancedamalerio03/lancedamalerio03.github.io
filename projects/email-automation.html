Project Overview

Advanced email automation system built specifically for Eskwelabs using n8n workflow automation, AI agents, and RAG technology to intelligently process incoming emails and generate contextually appropriate responses based on comprehensive strategic memos.

🏢 Built for Eskwelabs Operations

Developed during my Education Innovation Fellowship at Eskwelabs to revolutionize email communication workflows. The system leverages comprehensive strategic memos covering all Eskwelabs programs (DSF, DAB, EIF, Learning Sprints) as the knowledge base for intelligent response generation.

This solution addresses the challenge of maintaining consistent, accurate, and contextually relevant email responses across multiple educational programs while reducing manual workload by over 80%.

The system employs a sophisticated multi-agent architecture built on n8n automation platform, integrating AI-powered email categorization, RAG-based context retrieval from Eskwelabs strategic memos, and intelligent response generation. The workflow seamlessly connects Google Gmail APIs, vector database storage, and Discord notifications for comprehensive email management.

By utilizing advanced prompt engineering and context-aware AI agents, the platform ensures responses maintain Eskwelabs' brand voice while providing accurate information about bootcamps, fellowships, learning sprints, admissions processes, and program details. The system delivers contextually appropriate responses in under 2 minutes, transforming email communication efficiency.
